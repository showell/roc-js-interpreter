-- Short terms the x86-64 backend's Roc names may hold though no word list
-- has them, one a line, each with its reason after `--`: the words of the
-- Intel and AMD manuals, which name the registers, the instructions and the
-- parts of an encoding so, and which a reader of the encoder looks up there.
-- tools/lints.shale hands it to find_names_and_docs_that_say_too_little
-- (`accepted=`), through x86/lints.shale.

rdi    -- a general register, by the manual's name
rsp    -- the stack pointer, by the manual's name
rbp    -- the frame pointer, by the manual's name
xmm    -- the SSE registers, by the manual's name

imm    -- an immediate operand, as the manual's opcode tables abbreviate it

mem    -- a memory operand, as the manual's opcode tables abbreviate it

abi    -- the System V ABI, ELF's name for Linux's calling convention
len    -- Roc's own field, `List.sublist`'s `{ start, len }`
